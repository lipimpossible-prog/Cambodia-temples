Cambodia Temples

  • Angkor Wat

    Angkor Wat, the magnificent temple complex in Cambodia, continues to captivate visitors from around the world with its stunning architecture and rich history.

  • Phnom Bakheng

    Phnom Bakheng, a hilltop temple in Cambodia, offers breathtaking views of the Angkor Wat complex and is a popular spot for sunrise photography.

  • Prasat Ta Phrom

    Prasat Ta Phrom, a stunning temple in Cambodia, is known for its intricate carvings and historical significance.

  • Prasat Banteay Srey

    Banteay Srei is a 10th-century Hindu temple near Angkor, Cambodia, renowned for its intricate carvings in pink sandstone, earning it the title “Jewel of Khmer Art.”

  • Koh Ker

    Koh Ker, an ancient temple complex in Cambodia, is known for its impressive architecture and historical significance.
